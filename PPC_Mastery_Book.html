Chapter 01

What is PPC? PPC Kya Hai?

Understanding PPC

PPC stands for Pay-Per-Click. It is a digital advertising model where advertisers pay a fee each time one of their ads is clicked. You are buying visits to your site, rather than earning them organically.

When someone searches on Google, the top results are often paid ads. When you scroll Facebook or Instagram, many posts are paid advertisements.

Key Concepts

  • CPC (Cost Per Click) — How much you pay per click
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate) — % of people who see and click your ad
  • Quality Score — Google's 1-10 relevance rating
  • Conversion Rate — % of clicks that become sales
  • ROAS — Revenue earned per rupee spent
  • Impressions — Times your ad was shown
  • Reach — Unique people who saw your ad

Types of PPC

TypeWhereBest For
Search AdsGoogle search resultsServices, B2B
Display AdsWebsites (banners)Awareness, retargeting
Shopping AdsProduct ads with imagesE-commerce
Video AdsYouTubeStorytelling
Social AdsFacebook, InstagramTargeted campaigns
App AdsPlay Store, SearchApp installs
हिंदी में

PPC = Pay-Per-Click. Har click ke liye paisa lagta hai. Aap website pe visitors khareed rahe ho.

Google search ke top results = ads. Facebook/Instagram pe jo posts dikhte hain = paid ads.

Seekhna hoga: CPC, CTR, Quality Score, Conversion Rate, ROAS, Impressions, Reach.

Types: Search, Display, Shopping, Video, Social, App — har ek ka alag use hai.

⏱ Learning Time

2-3 Days — Concepts samajh lo.

Quality Score — Game Changer

Google's 1-10 rating. Higher score = Lower CPC. If your score is 10 and competitor's is 5, you pay HALF for same position. Aim for 7+.

Three Factors:

  • Expected CTR — How likely people click
  • Ad Relevance — How relevant ad is to keywords
  • Landing Page Experience — How good your page is

Keyword Match Types

TypeExampleReachTargeting
Broadrunning shoesWidestLeast targeted
Phrase"running shoes"ModerateBalanced
Exact[running shoes]NarrowestMost targeted
⚠️ Negative Keywords

Words you DON'T want to show for. Add 50-100 negatives to prevent budget waste.

H2: 3X More Leads Guaranteed

H3: Free Consultation Today

Add winners as keywords, losers as negatives

Ad Copy Refresh

Update every 2-3 months

3

Bid Adjustments

Increase for winners, decrease for losers

4

Budget Pacing

Spread evenly, increase during peaks

Budget Management Smart Spending

Principles

  • Start small, scale winners — ₹200-500/day start
  • 80/20 Rule — 80% proven, 20% testing
  • Budget Pacing — Don't blow budget in week 1
  • 10% Reserve — For opportunities

Chapter 16

50 Interview Questions Crack It

PPC Basics

What is PPC?
Pay-Per-Click — you pay each time someone clicks your ad. Buying traffic instead of earning organically.
PPC vs SEO vs SEM?
PPC = Paid ads. SEO = Organic search. SEM = PPC + SEO combined.
What is CTR?
Click-Through Rate = (Clicks/Impressions) × 100. Affects Quality Score and ad rank.
What is Quality Score?
Google's 1-10 rating based on CTR, ad relevance, landing page. Higher = lower CPC.

Google Ads

What is Ad Rank?
Max CPC Bid × Quality Score. Determines ad position.
What are negative keywords?
Excluded keywords that prevent ads from showing for irrelevant searches. Saves budget.
What is remarketing?
Showing ads to people who visited but didn't convert. Very effective.
What are ad extensions?
Extra info with ads: sitelinks, phone, location. Free to add, improve CTR.

Optimization Scenarios

CPC is too high. What do you do?
Check Quality Score, review keyword relevance, test new ad copy, add long-tail keywords.
No conversions. What do you check?
Conversion tracking, landing page, ad relevance, audience targeting, keyword intent.
Budget running out fast?
Reduce bids, pause poor performers, add negatives, adjust schedule.
High impressions, low clicks?
Ad copy not compelling, low Quality Score, wrong targeting. Test new headlines.
हिंदी में

Basics: PPC, CTR, Quality Score. Google Ads: Ad Rank, Negative Keywords, Remarketing, Extensions. Scenarios: CPC zyada? Conversions nahi? Budget khatam? — inke answers practice karo.
