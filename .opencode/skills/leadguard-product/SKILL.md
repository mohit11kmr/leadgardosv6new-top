# LeadGuard OS V6 — Product Identity & Scope

LeadGuard OS is an enterprise-grade revenue intelligence and website diagnostic system engineered to detect and prevent lead leakage across commercial web properties.

## Core Identity & Pillars

LeadGuard is defined strictly by five interconnected pillars:

1. **Website Diagnostic Engine**
   - High-fidelity crawling and multi-dimensional analysis of lead capture forms, call-to-action (CTA) paths, third-party analytics tags, tracking scripts, and network endpoints.
   - Real-world browser execution and headless interaction testing.

2. **Lead Leakage Detection**
   - Direct identification of points where prospective customer revenue is lost: broken forms, unhandled submission errors, silent webhook failures, untracked conversion events, SSL/DNS drop-offs, slow endpoint response times, and missing form validation feedback.

3. **Revenue Intelligence & Quantified Opportunity Loss**
   - Mathematical modeling of lost opportunity cost based on verified site traffic, average lead value, and industry conversion baselines.
   - Executive-ready financial impact assessments that translate technical defects into quantified business exposure.

4. **Continuous Watchdog & Anomaly Alerting**
   - Scheduled automated re-auditing, health checks, regression detection, uptime/endpoint monitoring, and immediate alerting via webhooks, email, and notification feeds when conversion paths degrade.

5. **Agency & Multi-Tenant Operating Platform**
   - Native multi-tenancy, white-label client reporting, shareable public audit views, agency portfolio management, branded client portal views, and automated remediation generation.

---

## Anti-Patterns & Prohibited Reductions

LeadGuard must **NEVER** be reduced, simplified, or rebranded into:

- ❌ **Generic SEO Checker**: Do not fill UI with generic meta-tag checkers, keyword density scores, or shallow search engine ranking advice. LeadGuard is focused on conversion infrastructure and revenue protection.
- ❌ **Generic Website Scanner**: Do not treat LeadGuard as a generic linter or simple curl/ping tool. Every finding must connect technical failure to lead loss, conversion failure, or operational risk.
- ❌ **Generic SaaS Landing Page / Template**: Do not treat screens as generic placeholder dashboards. Every data point, visualization, and flow must reflect real diagnostic telemetry, scan results, or customer intelligence.
